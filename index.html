Our Story

How it began

We met during O-Week, working yellow shirts in the same squad. Vivien's first impression of David: overly social and always late. David's first impression of Vivien: striking, but intimidatingly composed and hard to approach — meanwhile, David had a strong farmer look about him, complete with check shirts and rolled-up sleeves.

We got to know each other properly as first-year green juniors. Along with Jenny, we were the only first years in the squad, and we had similar interests. Over the next year, David made an effort — case competitions became his excuse to spend more time with her. He took her to restaurants that were several levels nicer than what most second years would attempt. It was clear he was serious.

Since then, we've built a life around travel. Last-minute hotel bookings, flights on a whim, getting lost in cities we'd never planned to visit. After a hike through the Bernese Oberland, David proposed by the lake in Lucerne. We can't wait to celebrate with you.

While You're In Town

Make A Trip Of It

I.

Opera House & Botanic Garden

A scenic walk from the venue along the harbour, past the Opera House and into the Royal Botanic Garden.

II.

The Rocks Markets

Weekend markets right outside our doorstep — local art, food stalls, and live music.

III.

Bondi to Coogee Walk

One of Sydney's best coastal walks, about 30 minutes from the city by taxi.
